# Mostafa & Basmala — Interactive Digital Wedding Invitation

A standalone, luxury interactive digital wedding invitation featuring a full-screen 3D embossed champagne envelope with a metallic antique-gold wax seal, smooth opening choreography, live 1-second countdown, `.ics` calendar export, Google Maps venue directions, honest RSVP confirmation with WhatsApp delivery, and full English / Arabic RTL switching.

## Customize Couple Details
Edit `WEDDING_CONFIG` at the top of `app.js` to change couple names, wedding date/time, couple portrait, venue address, Google Maps URL, and RSVP WhatsApp number.
