# Mostafa & Basmala — Interactive Digital Wedding Invitation

A standalone luxury interactive digital wedding invitation featuring a full-screen cinematic video cover with an Apple iOS Liquid Glass CTA, matte-frosted Liquid Glass interior cards styled in Burgundy Champagne Luxury, looping background wedding music with an adaptive floating controller, live 1-second synchronized countdown, `.ics` calendar export, Google Maps venue directions, local-preview RSVP with WhatsApp delivery, and full English / Arabic RTL switching.

## Customize Couple Details
Edit `WEDDING_CONFIG` at the top of `app.js` to update couple names, wedding date/time, couple portrait, venue address, Google Maps URL, and RSVP WhatsApp number.
